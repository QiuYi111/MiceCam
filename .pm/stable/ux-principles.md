# UX Principles

## 1. Content First
Camera previews are the hero. UI chrome is minimal and recedes. Every pixel of chrome must justify its existence against the preview area it occupies.

## 2. One-Click Recording
Global record button records ALL connected cameras. No per-camera toggles. The user's mental model is "record the experiment," not "manage camera states."

## 3. Clarity at a Glance
Status colors (green/amber/red) communicate system health instantly. Numbers are always paired with context (fps with expected value, drops with percentage).

## 4. No Surprises
Every destructive action confirms. Every failure explains itself. Every alert shows cause + action. Silent failures are the worst possible outcome.

## 5. Apple HIG Compliance
SF fonts, SF Symbols, HIG spacing/radius/touch targets. Navigation follows platform conventions. The app should feel at home on macOS.
